Good to know

Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?

Eldoret and Nairobi orders arrive in 1 to 2 working days. Other counties take 2 to 4 days. Delivery is free on orders over KES 5,000.

Can I pay with M-Pesa?

Yes. Enter your M-Pesa number at checkout and approve the prompt on your phone with your PIN.

What if my shade is wrong?

Use our Shade Finder first. If it still isn’t right, unopened or lightly swatched items can be exchanged within 14 days.

Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?

Yes. Every formula is 100% plant-derived and never tested on animals.

How do discount codes work?

Enter your code in the bag. Join the Journal below to get WELCOME10 for 10% off your first order.
